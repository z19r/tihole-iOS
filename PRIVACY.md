# tihole privacy policy

tihole does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app talks only to the Pi-hole servers you add. Nothing is sent to the
  developer or to any third party.
- There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or
  tracking.
- The Pi-hole addresses and names you enter are saved on your device. Each
  Pi-hole's password is saved in the iOS Keychain on your device and is
  only ever sent to that Pi-hole.
- Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/z19r/tihole-iOS/issues>.
