tihole is a native client for Pi-hole v6. See what every device on your
network is looking up, pause ad blocking for a few minutes, and manage your
lists from your iPhone or iPad, on as many Pi-holes as you run.

DASHBOARD
• Total queries, blocked queries, active clients and gravity size, live
• CPU, memory, temperature and load at a glance, with health warnings
• Queries over time, query types, upstreams, top domains and top clients

BLOCKING
• Turn blocking off for 5, 10 or 15 minutes, or until you turn it back on
• A live countdown in every tab shows when blocking comes back

QUERY LOG
• A live log of every DNS query, with a domain filter
• Swipe a query to allow or block its domain

LISTS
• Allow and deny domains, exact or regex, with groups
• Adlists, with a streamed gravity update
• Groups, clients and local DNS records (A, AAAA and CNAME)

SETTINGS AND SYSTEM
• Search and edit Pi-hole's full FTL configuration
• System info, diagnosis messages, network devices and the DNS log
• Restart DNS, flush logs and flush the network table

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Talks only to the Pi-holes you add. No accounts, analytics or tracking.
• Passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.
• Works with HTTPS, self-signed certificates and plain HTTP on your LAN.

tihole needs Pi-hole v6 or later and an app password from its web
interface. tihole is an independent app and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Pi-hole LLC.
