Privacy policy

tihole for iOS does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app talks only to the Pi-hole servers you add. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking in the app.
  • The Pi-hole names and addresses you enter are saved on your device. Each Pi-hole's password is saved in the iOS Keychain on your device and is only ever sent to that Pi-hole.
  • What the app shows (queries, clients, settings) comes from your Pi-hole and stays between your device and your Pi-hole.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

This website

This site may count page views with Umami, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. Your theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage.
